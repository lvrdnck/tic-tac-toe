# Tic-Tac-Toe Game

A feature-rich browser-based tic-tac-toe game with comprehensive gameplay, analysis, and training features.

## Features

### Core Gameplay
- **Single-player (AI Mode)**: Play against AI with three difficulty levels (Easy, Smart, Perfect)
- **Two-player (Local Mode)**: Local multiplayer with persistent round tracking
- **Play as X or O**: Choose which symbol to use in AI mode

### Advanced Features
- **Move History & Undo**: Full move history with undo functionality
- **Hint System**: Get AI suggestions for optimal moves
- **Coach Mode**: Real-time feedback on move quality and strategy
- **Trainer/Puzzle Mode**: Tactical puzzle challenges
- **Achievement System**: Unlock badges for accomplishments
- **Profile & Stats**: Track wins, losses, draws, accuracy, and achievements
- **Strategic Analysis**: Best move recommendations, game outlook, threat detection
- **Multiple Themes**: Light, arcade, and paper color schemes
- **Sound Effects**: Audio feedback for game events
- **Celebration Animations**: Confetti effects for wins

## Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. No server or build process needed
3. Start playing!

## Game Modes

### Play vs AI
Choose your difficulty level and play against the computer:
- **Easy**: AI makes random moves
- **Smart**: AI uses basic strategy
- **Perfect**: Unbeatable AI using optimal moves

### Two Player
Play locally with another person on the same device with round tracking.

### Trainer
Test your skills with tactical puzzle challenges.

## How to Play

1. Click on an empty cell to make your move
2. The AI (in single-player mode) will respond with its move
3. Get three in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to win
4. Use the **Hint** button for AI suggestions
5. Use the **Undo** button to take back your last move
6. Click **New Game** to reset and start over

## Game Stats & Profile

Track your performance across all game modes:
- Total wins, losses, and draws
- Accuracy percentage
- Unlocked achievements
- Progress across different game modes
